## 🔬 Research Focus

My work bridges three traditionally separate areas:

- **Risk‑Sensitive Control** — analytic CVaR formulations inside real‑time MPC, with provably no additional NLP decision variables
- **Multi‑Agent RL** — risk‑aware MAPPO with dual‑head CVaR critics, GP‑uncertainty attention, and hierarchical Lyapunov‑stable substrates
- **Distributed Gaussian Processes** — Bayesian Committee Machine fusion for cooperative perception under uncertainty
